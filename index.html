About

I'm Álvaro, a web developer and logistics specialist based in Valencia. I spent 17 years at ISTOBAL working with warehouse management systems (WMS), ERP and PDA devices, and 12 years before that programming CNC and laser machine tools.

In 2026 I finished the Higher Vocational Degree in Web Application Development (DAW). I build with Laravel, React, MySQL and Docker, and my final project, AgroGestión, is a web app for managing an agricultural holding.

I also run my own citrus and persimmon farm in the Ribera Alta, where I organise and direct the work of several people.
